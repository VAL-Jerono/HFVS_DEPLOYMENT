    Resource Allocation Optimisation

    What binds: county delivery capacity, not levy revenue

    The 6,000-unit programme requires KSh 19.75B (27% of annual levy revenue). Most funded counties are constrained by municipal delivery ceilings rather than financial capital. Recommended: Option C (Capacity-Targeted) to expand local delivery capacity while maintaining equity.

    Regime A vs Regime B allocation comparison

    Regime A concentrates units in top-capacity hubs (26 counties activated); Regime B mandates universal coverage across all 47 counties.

    Binding constraint breakdown (Regime A)

    Diagnosis of active constraints preventing further unit expansion across counties.

    Diagnostic categories explained
